# Your local workspace

Create a project from the welcome screen or open existing `.editbay` work. New
and Open keep your other tabs intact. Click a file to open it. Hold Shift to
start selecting several files, then choose Open selected. Search finds names and
paths. Projects follows existing `.omabrand` folders, including nested projects.
New projects choose picture dimensions and an exact rational frame rate. The
workspace supports 128 open projects and reads project files up to 8 MiB. Media
and timeline authoring arrive through the later roadmap milestones.

Save writes your current revision. Save as chooses a new destination and retains
the previous file. Save a copy creates a new independent project and keeps your
opened document unchanged. Existing destinations are protected. If another writer
changes or removes your saved file, save reports the conflict instead of replacing
their work. Save as or recovery can rescue your version into a new file.

Rename is one reversible command. Undo and redo keep document revisions moving
forward. Ctrl+N creates work, Ctrl+O opens files, Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Shift+S chooses
a new destination, Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Closing dirty work offers
Save, Discard or Cancel.
Settings lets you change these Ctrl+letter commands. Clipboard letters remain
reserved, and text fields keep their own undo history.

Every dirty tab, including inactive and untitled work, prepares recovery after
one second idle or ten seconds of continued changes. Recovery is separate from a
manual save. The workspace reports checkpoint errors and keeps older intact work
available. Recovered on the welcome screen shows valid history and corrupt files.
Choose a revision, inspect its sequence settings, then recover into a new file.
Your original remains intact. No account or network connection is needed.

Settings chooses welcome or restoration of saved tabs at startup. Workspace,
catalog folder and shortcuts save atomically in EditBay's own local state
directory. Corrupt settings stay intact and show a reload action. A competing
settings writer must reload before saving. Untitled work returns through Recovered.

Brand bank finds the active project's shared `.omabrand` context, or lets you
choose/create a bank. Add original fonts, logos, artwork, title packages or LUTs;
save client/project details and palettes; export an exact asset copy. Existing
names and destination files are protected. Refresh after an outside edit. See
[shared bank rules](SHARED_BRANDS.md) for formats, limits and ownership.
Asset operations and refresh keep your unsaved bank fields. Changes made while
details are saving stay dirty. Save or discard those details before choosing
another bank or closing EditBay.

The palette and typography follow your Omarchy desktop. Work stays local until
you explicitly choose an available sharing workflow.
