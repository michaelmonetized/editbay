# Migration, adoption, and retention

The mission is to remove production dependencies that keep people on macOS or
Windows. Sell a completed professional workflow and prove it with their work.
Rust, a feature list, and attractive screenshots alone do not establish switching
value. Omadesign's existing audience and cross-app assets supply the first channel.

## Bring your work

The native welcome should make Open existing work as easy to find as New.
Migration starts with one real project and reports whether the artist can deliver
it without returning to their old production setup.

| Source dependency | Acquisition route | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FCP libraries/cuts | FCPXML import, connected-edit habits, fonts/assets, shortcuts | Timings, nests, roles, titles, effects and missing/baked features reported |
| Premiere projects | Supported XML/OTIO pathways, presets and dependency inventory | Sync, stream/channel maps, retimes, captions, relinks and output comparison |
| Resolve finishing | Supported interchange/reconform, LUTs/grades where representable | Color transforms, grades retained/baked/missing, source timecode and deliverables |
| After Effects work | Native project feasibility plus composition/asset interchange and baking routes | Editable versus baked structure, fonts, expressions/plugins and timing report |
| Photoshop/Illustrator/design | Omadesign's proven native layered/vector codecs and shared artwork | Layers/text/path/effects fidelity and explicit conversion notes |
| Client/team pipeline | Shared projects/brands, review links, templates and role-based delivery | Client acceptance and collaboration with reversible exports |
| Platform-specific plugins/hardware | Inventory dependencies; compatible Linux plugins, replacements and adapters | Every required dependency works or its limitation is explicit |

The Rust file format is versioned. Import reads originals, converts to a separate
destination, and reports dropped/converted capabilities. Do not silently mutate
source projects or call XML interchange full native-project compatibility.

## First-run experience

Use Omadesign's welcome/catalog conventions: Your Work, Recovered, central
creation/help, and Projects. Show familiar thumbnails, client/project context,
shared brand assets, and team work only when it exists. Offer named shortcut
presets and a practical migration guide. Local authoring/recovery require no login.

An optional Connect Oma account flow links existing teams/clients/assets with
explicit user action. Cloud first appears when the artist wants sharing, review,
or remote storage. Provide template and native agent-assisted creation paths,
with version-matched docs and editable outputs.

## Retention through accumulated value

- A portable brand/project bank compounds fonts, marks, palettes, LUTs, titles,
  reusable animation rigs, client delivery profiles, and approved templates.
- Omadesign artwork stays editable when used in EditBay; updates propagate through
  versioned dependencies with preview/approval rather than arbitrary overwrites.
- Client review history, source versions, project organization and team libraries
  reduce repeated setup across jobs and applications.
- Saved roto corrections, tracks, rigs, presets and render assets shorten the next
  project. They remain useful even when a model/runtime changes.
- Consistent shortcuts, recovery, welcome, and workspace behavior build familiarity.
- Keep native files/documentation and supported interchange exports available.
  Never require a subscription/cloud response just to open or render local work.

This is the lock-in strategy: make the accumulated working environment valuable
enough to keep using while maintaining a practical way to leave. Do not recreate
the platform barriers the product is supposed to remove.

## Acquisition by completed workflow

| Cohort | Demonstration | Release gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Omadesign creators | Existing brand artwork → editable video → client review | R1–R3 plus validated asset exchange/review |
| Commercial/social editors | Migrated paid spot, revisions, variants, clean delivery | R3; relevant R6/R9 capabilities |
| Interview/event editors | Sync, multicam, transcripts, captions, sound and handoff | R6; applicable R8 |
| Motion designers | Typography, expressions/rigs, editable brand film and templates | R5 |
| VFX artists | Auto-roto corrections, screen replacement, cleanup and tracked graphics | R4/R5; R10 for 3D work |
| Colorists/audio professionals | Managed finish/reconform or sample-accurate mix/stems | R7/R8 |

Invite existing Omadesign users through an explicitly authorized, opt-in campaign.
Interview their actual work/dependencies and use their permissioned projects for
evaluation. Do not assume the whole audience is the same cohort or send messages
automatically as part of implementing this roadmap.

Publish migration guides, supported-format/plugin/hardware matrices, job-focused
videos, editable example projects, and before/after time measurements. AI marketing
shows the correction and revision flow, export, and saved editable result. A model
demo with an ideal shot is insufficient product proof.

Clients reviewing a shared project can discover EditBay/Oma through the actual
workflow; account creation must follow role needs rather than obstruct viewing.
Showcase/templates/community grow only from published work and contributor consent.

## Measure readiness

Track installation → first imported/migrated project → saved/recovered project →
verified deliverable → real client revision → second job → team reuse.
Use consented product telemetry and explicit interviews; no private footage upload
as an analytics side effect. Measure conversion losses, assistance needed, crash/
recovery success, correction time, playback stability, and retention per cohort.

A launch gate requires independent users finishing and repeating jobs on supported
Omarchy machines, with revisions and handoffs accepted. Collect willingness to
pay if a paid offer is planned. Storage/cloud/compute pricing and entitlement are
separate product decisions; do not silently impose them through local file access.

## Remaining production dependencies

The first acceptance jobs cover video postproduction, motion, VFX, color, sound,
and connected design assets. Some users also depend on music composition, live
production, deep 3D authoring, or photo-library workflows. Keep these in the
migration dependency inventory; develop or integrate additional capabilities as
the relevant cohorts are qualified. A handoff to an existing Linux tool needs
actual editable interchange and job evidence. A user is not qualified to switch
until their entire required production workflow works on Omarchy.
